Tile coordinates in the inspector start at 1. Terrain heights are simulation steps; the Terraced preset uses levels 0, 2, 4, and 6. The crowd demonstration retains 37 path tiles at height 8 and nine original track tiles at height 0. The initial demonstration circuit's rails range from height 8 to 16. The 0.13.0 connection contract joins only compatible opposing cardinal edges at the same endpoint height; ramps have no side entrance. The interface reports guests/lost, seeking/queued/returning, cancellation events, queue capacity, oldest wait, passenger phases, completed trips, current ticket price, affordability, ride revenue, guest funds and park cash. Guests are individually inspectable through the Guests list. Guest/train world meshes remain unpickable; their supporting track/path surfaces are selectable.

Flat terrain exposes the same track that Terraced terrain buries. Switching presets preserves elapsed time, materials, object identities, and object heights. Underground view removes terrain from rendering and picking; its reference floor is decorative and cannot be selected. Hidden selections retain their identity but do not show through occluding surfaces.

P selects public-path construction; J selects a queue for the ride chosen in Rides [Y]. C cycles Flat and the four world directions of rise, retaining Queue mode when active. Slopes use low height 0–29 and conservative clearance 3; Flat uses height 0–30/clearance 2. Matching opposing endpoints connect; ramps have no side entry. I/Gate places a Flat entrance at ride height facing a lateral coaster station side or an outer carousel body tile, and C rotates its facing. Preview/commit share validation. X removes visible paths or gates one piece per deliberate click. R restores public fixture paths without replacing player pieces. The ride panel requires one unbranched, acyclic owned queue from the gate to exactly one public edge. Ready means connected topology. With the ride open, guests approach on public paths and join only at the public queue end. Capacity is one admitted guest per queue tile; approaching guests reserve no place. Guests walk in ticket order without overtaking, including on slopes. K/Exit adds a station-side exit with a public landing connected to the queue-entry component. This enables passenger journeys; path and gate construction remains free.

B/Raise and V/Lower target the picked terrain column, including a visible cliff face. Each click changes height by one step within 0–32 while preserving Grass/Sand material. Water and Underground terrain editing are blocked. Green plus/blue minus indicators show the direction; red X indicates rejection. Paths, rails, and guests stay in place, and invalid clearance leaves the park unchanged. Successful edits display Custom; T is an explicit preset reset. No terrain brushes, water editing, terrain slopes, costs, or refunds are included.

The displayed timer is elapsed simulation time, not a park calendar. Trees and the entrance arch remain decorative. Every launch starts a fresh seeded demonstration; use Load to continue a saved park. Startup does not overwrite an existing save.

With the default no-minimum loading policy, the empty train retains its automatic station/lap behavior. With a valid connected exit and ready open queue, guests can reserve six seats, board over 60 ticks, ride a real lap, and exit over 60 ticks onto the public landing. Passenger transfers and finished-lap occupants hold the train while they resolve. Closing in flight finishes the occupied lap; a missing exit holds returned passengers until repair. Occupied circuit track and active transfer paths/portals reject demolition atomically. Space freezes train, queue and transfers. The 60-second queue patience, tail-first cancellation return and ten-second retry cooldown remain for queue visitors. Completed trips count only real lap completion followed by public return; a paid fare is earned at departure, a separate event. The train uses tuned integer motion rather than a realistic coaster physics model.

New parks give each guest $20 and the park $1,000; tickets start free. Guests must afford the current fare when seeking/admitting and again at the queue front. Boarding locks the price without debiting the wallet. The first actual departure charges that locked amount once, increases ride revenue and park cash, and records guest spending. A later price change cannot reprice a reserved passenger. Predeparture cancellation costs nothing, so there is no payment to refund; closing during a lap keeps the fare earned. Free rides earn zero. Service costs now reduce park cash. Carousel construction also reduces cash; demolition refunds part of its paid cost. Wallet replenishment, loans and park admission fees remain absent.

Guests differ in price comfort ($4/$6/$8/$10) and queue comfort (15/25/35/45 seconds). Refusals distinguish insufficient money from poor value. Crossing queue comfort reduces satisfaction once; a cancelled joined visit gives a separate penalty and no review. A completed physical return gives one 1–5-star review based on the locked fare and actual queue wait, then updates satisfaction. Historical trips migrated from old saves stay unrated. The Guests panel explains traits, current activity, thoughts and review history, while blocking edits behind it. See [the guest experience report](docs/GUEST_EXPERIENCE_REPORT.md) for exact rules.

M/Services builds original food stalls ($120), drink stalls ($80) and toilets ($100). Removing a paid facility returns half its recorded cost ($60/$40/$50), once; old free facilities return $0. The quote validates geometry, cash and capacity without changes, and a rejected purchase changes nothing. Deliberate paused edits still charge/refund at the current tick. Place a one-tile building at height 0–29, facing outward toward a matching public-path edge. Guests seek reachable, open, affordable facilities for needs of 60 or more, physically walk there, wait for a single customer slot and use it for 90 ticks. Starting use locks the price; completion alone charges once, resets the relevant need to 20 and adds five satisfaction points. Closing, demolition or lost access cancels unfinished visits without charge or relief. Demolished facility records retain earned income. Defaults are food $2, drink $1 and toilet free, editable within $0–$100. Needs grow on simulation ticks, and crossing critical 85 costs five satisfaction points once per crossing. Service visitors finish before seeking a ride; unmet needs do not stop ordinary walking/rides. See [the guest services report](docs/GUEST_SERVICES_REPORT.md) for timing, accounting and a reproducible build layout. Stock, merchandise, energy and nausea remain later work.
N/Finance displays cash, ride/service income, construction/running/supplies costs, demolition refunds, net result and paged transactions. Open Food/Drinks/Toilets cost $6/$4/$2 per simulation minute, charged $0.30/$0.20/$0.10 every 90 open ticks; completed uses cost $0.60/$0.25/$0.10 in supplies. Open disconnected facilities still cost money. Closing preserves the partial cost interval and stops its clock; pausing stops all simulation costs. A sale can cover its own supplies. Insufficient park funds closes the facility without customer charge or relief; reopen manually after funds become available. Removed facilities keep their income/expense history. The [finance report](docs/SERVICE_FINANCE_REPORT.md) explains the ledger and retained history.

Rides → Edit coaster track (or Z) opens eight piece choices: Station, Straight, Left curve, Right curve, Chain lift, Drop, Steep drop and Brake. C rotates the incoming world heading; height selects the entry endpoint. Lifts rise one step, drops descend one or two, and other pieces stay level. Click to place or replace a draft tile; matching opposite endpoints must close one loop. Use Copy live, Undo, Redo, Designs, Erase, Clear, Apply or Done. Apply requires a closed, stopped coaster with no active visitors and preserves its installed circuit on failure. Drafts survive Save/Load. Chain lifts hold a fixed speed, drops accelerate and brakes slow the train using the existing approximate motion rules. Moving the station may require reconnecting gates. Drafting and Clear remain free. New pieces cost Station $100, Straight $10, Curve $15, Lift $25, Drop −1 $15, Drop −2 $20 and Brake $20. The quote shows purchases, refunds, net payment and current/remaining cash. Exact retained pieces preserve IDs and paid basis; replaced or removed paid pieces refund 50% once. Inspect a live rail and use Delete to remove it with the displayed refund; passengers block demolition. Remaining paid rails retain their refund after a circuit fault. N/Finance records Track construction and Track refund separately. Further geometry and additional coasters remain later work.

Copy live overwrites the current plan with the installed circuit's exact grades and piece directions, without changing the live ride. Undo/Redo keeps up to 64 draft edits in the current session: placement, replacement, erase, Clear, Copy live and changed design loads. A new edit after Undo discards the redo branch. Refused actions and unchanged plans add no history. Done, Escape or closing/reopening the editor retains the plan and history; successful Apply clears both history stacks, while a refused Apply keeps them. Saving stores the current draft but not its history. Successful Load/Backup starts with empty history. Undo/Redo restores draft contents only; it never reverses purchases, refunds, installed rails or guest activity. Restored drafts may be incomplete or blocked by later world changes, and Apply validates them again.

Ctrl+D or Designs opens a library of up to 32 named designs. Save new keeps a nonempty draft of up to 256 pieces, including unfinished layouts; names support 32 Unicode characters after trimming. Names are compared using Unicode lowercase, and duplicates are refused. Update selected and Delete selected require confirmation and reject a design changed by another instance. These library changes are outside draft Undo/Redo.

**Import file** opens the native Windows picker for one version-1 `.parktrack.json` design. A duplicate library name becomes a new Unicode-safe numbered name, such as `Loop (2)`; no existing design is overwritten. Strict UTF-8/JSON validation, a 128 KiB file limit, regular-file checks and library locking reject unsupported or unsafe input. Cancellation and refused transfers preserve the catalog, draft and park.

**Export selected** opens the native Windows Save picker and exports the exact selected saved design, including its name and ordered geometry. The current draft and placement translation/rotation are not exported. The picker confirms overwriting the chosen destination; export then flushes a sibling temporary file and atomically replaces only that `.parktrack.json` file. Suggested filenames sanitize unsafe characters and Windows device names. Import/export does not build rails or change draft history: use **Load into draft** for an undoable draft replacement and **Apply** for construction.

The read-only check reports purchases, refunds, payment or receipt, current cash and readiness, or a placement refusal. An incomplete or obstructed plan can still be loaded for editing when its structural bounds are valid. Browsing, previews and quotes never alter the live park or current draft; loading remains one free Undo step when changed, and Apply validates again.

Choose a saved design, adjust X/Y, entry height and clockwise rotation, then Load into draft. The anchor is its first station, or its first piece if there is no station. Loading preserves piece order and grades, changes only the draft, and creates one Undo step when different; an identical load preserves redo. Apply still checks the actual park, connection, clearance and available cash before buying or replacing live rails. Loading a design never transfers money, paid cost basis, ride IDs or passengers.

Y/Rides selects the active ride or starts Build carousel. The 3 × 3 footprint reserves three height steps and starts closed; six seats run a 720-tick cycle, with a 90-tick station wait and 60-tick transfers. Add its own queue [J], entrance [I] and public-connected exit [K], then open it. The round body needs no orientation; gate facing still uses C. Only one carousel is active at a time, with eight lifetime records retained for history. Occupied rides reject demolition. Empty carousel removal removes its body and gates together but preserves owned queue paths and financial/trip history. Building costs $300; successful empty demolition refunds $150. The preview shows the quote, and insufficient cash rejects placement without changes. Carousels migrated from earlier saves cost nothing retrospectively and refund $0.

Guests choose reachable open rides using wallet/value acceptance, route length, queue length, fare, a deterministic coaster/carousel preference and prior completions; stable RideId breaks ties. Existing visitors keep their owned ride through queueing, transfer and the physical cycle. Ride reviews remain guest feedback and do not yet influence this choice score. The Guests panel identifies current activity and the selected ride's fare; the header shows total park income, and Finance identifies the earning ride.

## Test and operate rides

In Rides → Operations, minimum load is 0–6 passengers; 0 preserves the original no-minimum dispatch. Extra loading wait is 1–60 seconds, default 30. Mandatory station dwell runs first. The extra timer advances only at safe departure opportunities while the minimum is unmet; boarding, unloading, restarted station dwell and exit holds do not count. It is not a wall-clock deadline. Accumulated extra wait survives boarding's dwell reset. Timeout permits departure below the minimum while existing transfer/exit protections still apply. Reset validates both settings and policy before changing either.

Rides → Test measures an empty mechanics lap using the installed coaster's station wait and lift/brake settings. It excludes passenger loading policy and extra wait. Results use game tiles, tiles per second and terrain steps; layout estimates are separate from guest reviews and do not affect demand. Force telemetry and realistic physics remain absent. Changes to installed geometry/settings, opening or occupying the ride, selection changes or loading a save invalidate the result. Unapplied draft edits do not.

## Save and continue

Saves live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`, separate from the executable and shared OneDrive source folder. `park.park.json` is the primary slot; `park.park.json.bak` is the previous valid backup. Saving replaces the one slot, including player-built/removed paths and custom terrain heights. Backup loading is explicit, and a corrupt primary is never rotated into the backup. A successful load resets camera/selection/view and tool mode, pauses simulation, and resumes at 1x when requested; authoritative train and guest state is retained. Camera, tool height/ghost, UI settings, and pause/speed preference are not saved. A same-update Save includes the world click; Load/Backup takes precedence over pending edits even when loading fails.

Version 0.33.0 writes strict schema/content **17/17**, storing nondefault per-ride loading policies and accumulated extra wait alongside operating settings. Explicit readers migrate versions 1–16 with no-minimum policies and zero extra wait; the exact version-16 reader retains tuned station/cycle timing. Versions 0.32 and earlier reject schema 17, so retain older save copies before saving with 0.33. Loading alone never rewrites old slots. Migration preserves original fields, drafts and financial history; old free rails receive no retroactive refund basis. The draft remains separate from installed rails. Undo/Redo stacks and test results are transient and clear on successful Load/Backup, even when loaded inputs match. Normal play remains 32 × 32 with existing save caps. Use an absolute isolated `--save-dir` for QA. [The testing and dispatch report](docs/RIDE_TESTING_DISPATCH_REPORT.md) records current migration verification; historical reports retain their named-build evidence.

Named designs use an independent version-1 format in `track-designs\track-designs.json` beneath the same save directory. They survive park Load/Backup and restarts, and are not part of a park snapshot or draft history. `--save-dir` isolates both parks and the library. Names never become filenames. Writes use a separate lock, flushed temporary files and a previous-catalog `.bak`; a damaged catalog is reported instead of being silently replaced. To transfer the library, close the game and copy its `track-designs` directory as well as the park slots. Recovery instructions are in the design library report.

Thumbnail and preview images are recreated from saved geometry after each restart. A bounded memory cache holds at most 32 thumbnails and one selected preview, replacing or releasing images after geometry updates or deletion. No image files are written to the library. Design/catalog format version 1 is unchanged. Search and sorting are transient presentation state, retained when reopening the panel but reset on restart; they never reorder the catalog or edit the park. Park schema/content 17/17 stores the separate operating settings, loading policies and elapsed extra wait.
