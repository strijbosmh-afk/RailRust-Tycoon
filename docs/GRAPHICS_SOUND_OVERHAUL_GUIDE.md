# Graphics and sound quality — 0.53.0

The park now has more surface detail at normal playing map sizes: shoreline bands follow the water's actual edges, grass and sand carry small material marks, soil cliffs show a lit rim and fine cracks, timber paths show grain and fasteners, and trees catch warm highlights. These details follow camera rotation and the terrain's real height. The large diagnostic maps keep simpler materials so this stage does not multiply their scene geometry.

Open **Help → Sound [F10]** to adjust Master, Music, Ambience, Rides and Alerts & interface independently. The original meadow theme now has two alternating phrases, harmony, bass shaping and a light answering arpeggio. Coaster sound follows its leading car; carousel sound follows the running ride. Panning or zooming the camera changes each ride's stereo position and distance smoothly. The sound controls still fade on focus loss, and ride sounds pause with the park while music and ambience continue.

The effects and score are generated by this project. No external soundtrack, artwork, font or asset pack is needed. Sound settings remain in a separate small `sound-settings.json` file; they never change park saves or finances. Park save schema/content remain **25/25**. Transfer both the primary save and its `.bak` with the game closed when moving player progress to another PC. The shared OneDrive source path and full rebuild steps are in [RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md](../RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md).
