# Ride operating costs — 0.39.0

Implementation is in verification on `codex/ride-operating-costs`, based on verified 0.38.0 checkpoint `a58d674`. Exact-source 0.37 CI 40182744749 passed all 38 steps; 0.38 CI 40183614031 passed all 39 steps. Earlier packages remain retained.

This bounded MGT-01/MGT-04 step charges each open coaster $1 and each open carousel $0.50 per 900 fixed ticks (30 simulation seconds). Closing/pausing stops accrual; partial periods persist. Ride IDs determine billing order before facility costs and departure income. A failed bill requests ordinary closure, preserving paid journeys and pending costs without debt. Cash recovery does not auto-open; player opening requires sufficient funds for an overdue bill and rechecks access. Reopening pays the overdue bill next tick.

`RideCostRecord` stores sorted bounded owner IDs, accrual start, partial interval, lifetime expense and funds-closure reason. The ledger adds `RideUpkeep` with exact owner and rate validation. Opening, archived and recent ride finance totals include required upkeep fields; lifetime totals reconcile with ride counters and park cash. Finance shows separate ride/facility upkeep and per-ride cashflows subtract operating expenses. Rides displays the rate, paid upkeep and persistent closure cause.
