# Park finance summary

Open **Finance [N]**, choose **Ride cashflows**, then **Park summary**. The view button cycles back to Transactions. The summary has one page; Previous/Next do not change its figures. It is read-only. Space pauses the simulation; viewing Finance alone does not pause costs or activity.

The summary shows lifetime recorded results in dollars:

| Figure | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Ride operating profit | Ride ticket income − ride upkeep |
| Service operating profit | Service sales − facility upkeep − supplies |
| Combined operating profit | Ride operating profit + service operating profit |
| Net cash | Combined operating profit + demolition/track refunds − construction |
| Current cash | Starting cash + net cash |

Operating profit can be positive while net cash is negative after building. A demolition refund increases cash without becoming operating income. Negative amounts show a minus sign; a zero or positive result shows a plus sign.

These are cumulative results, including removed rides and facilities. Older transactions rolled out of the recent history remain included. The starting cash is the park's original $1,000, not the balance when you last loaded or opened Finance. The displayed open-cost rate at the top is the current upkeep rate; it does not predict future profit or include future supplies purchases.

Older parks preserve their recorded income and expenses. Free legacy construction keeps a zero paid basis. Upgrading a pre-0.39 park does not invent retrospective ride upkeep. This read-only report keeps save schema/content **20/20** and does not add monthly accounts, asset values, loans, wages or new spending rules.
