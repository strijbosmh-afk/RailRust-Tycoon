# Scenario selection and Boardwalk Sprint — 0.69.0

Open **Guests [L] → Scenarios**. Your park begins in **Sandbox** with no scenario deadline or locked charging rule. The pane describes two original challenges and shows your current progress. Choose one once for this park. A challenge cannot be cancelled, restarted, or replaced in the same save; keep an earlier save if you may want to try another.

**Meadow Challenge** remains the untimed goal: 20 new visitors, 5 completed visitor ride visits, and $750 park cash at once. It locks **Free entry**, so ride tickets may still be charged. Radio ads are unavailable; local flyers remain available. A running radio campaign must finish before you can start Meadow.

**Boardwalk Sprint** gives you **ten simulation minutes** from the Start button to welcome **24 new visitors**, complete **12 visitor ride visits**, and hold at least **$900 park cash** on the same tick. It locks **Free rides**, resetting current ride ticket prices to zero and preventing future paid ride tickets. You may charge park entry and run local flyers or radio ads. A connected public-path entry, enabled arrivals, an open park, and accessible open rides are needed to make progress. Existing arrivals and completed ride visits do not count; the two counters start when the challenge begins. Cash includes loans, so this is a balance target, not a profit target.

The deadline is measured in running simulation ticks. Space pauses it. On the deadline tick, meeting all goals wins; otherwise the challenge is marked **FAILED**. A win or failure stays recorded even if the park changes later. The park remains playable after failure. The Scenarios pane shows the outcome, goals and remaining time. **Guests → Visitor flow** still controls entry, arrivals, flyers and radio; it also summarizes the active challenge. Clicking a locked charging action or choosing a second challenge gives a notice without changing the park.

Strict park saves use schema/content **39/39**. Version-38 Meadow saves load with their original progress and completion. Older sandbox saves remain sandbox. Older executables cannot read version-39 saves. Playable primary and `.bak` saves live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`; close the game and copy both to another PC. The exact shared OneDrive source path and build steps are in [RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md](../RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md). New parks still start flat with the coaster station at ground level.
