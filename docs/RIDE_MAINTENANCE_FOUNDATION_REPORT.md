# Ride maintenance foundation — 0.55.0

Each installed coaster and carousel gets a bounded, ordered maintenance record on the simulation clock. It stores the last completed-trip baseline, service count and service tick. The status is derived from the authoritative completed guest-trip counter: inspection at 120, shutdown at 1,200. There is no random breakdown draw. At shutdown the existing open/close operation stops new departures while an occupied trip can finish. The checked reopening command refuses an unserviced ride. Manual service requires a closed, stationary ride and resets its baseline without changing guest, ride finance, construction or scenery state. Existing recurring ride upkeep covers this service action.

Strict park saves advance to schema/content **26/26**. Version 25 and earlier parks migrate with no maintenance record; the first subsequent simulation step establishes the baseline from their current ride counters. Current saves require the bounded maintenance field, reject unknown ride owners and inconsistent counters, and reject a maintenance field smuggled into a version-25 save. Demolishing a carousel removes its live maintenance record. The 8 MiB save limit and existing ride and map caps remain in force.

The Operations panel exposes an inspection or shutdown notice and a Service ride action beside Reset defaults. The action is disabled while a ride is open, moving or still occupied. The feature is a foundation for the next staff phase, including mechanic assignment, service access and wage accounting. Staff do not yet walk or repair automatically.
