# Visitor wellness and Rest facilities

Open **Guests [L]** and select a visitor. **Energy** is better when high; **Nausea** is better when low. Walking spends one energy point every three simulated seconds. Waiting restores one point every six seconds. Nausea eases by one point every three seconds. Completing a coaster trip adds 22 nausea points; a carousel trip adds five. Visitors with energy at 25 or below, or nausea at 70 or above, wait before choosing another ride. An existing queue or ride visit still finishes under its normal rules.

Open **Services [M] → Build Rest** to place an open pavilion beside a public path. It costs **$140** to build, with a 50% demolition refund on its paid cost. Rest begins to attract visitors when energy is 40 or below, or nausea is 60 or above. A completed use takes three simulated seconds, restores energy to at least 90 and reduces nausea to at most 10, and adds five satisfaction points. The default guest price is free; the selected facility's F6/F7 controls can change it. A Rest facility costs **$0.25** every 90 open ticks and **$0.20** in supplies per completed use. Closing it stops future upkeep and cancels unfinished visits without charging the visitor.

**Guests → Park stats** now counts low energy and nausea among urgent needs. The **Urgent** guest-list filter includes energy at 30 or below and nausea at 70 or above, alongside hunger, thirst and toilet at 70 or above. The session chart uses the same expanded urgent definition. The exact visitor meters and numbers remain visible even if the park is paused.

Saves use strict schema/content **25/25**. Opening an older park preserves all original fields and starts each existing visitor at energy 80 and nausea 0; new visitors receive deterministic starting values. Save the park again to write the new format. The primary and `.bak` slots remain under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`; move both files together when continuing on another PC.
