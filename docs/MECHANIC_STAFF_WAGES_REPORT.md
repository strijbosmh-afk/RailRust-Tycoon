# Mechanic staffing and wages — 0.56.0

The park has one bounded mechanic employment state and a fixed-tick wage clock. Employment changes are deliberate and free. Every 900 employed simulation ticks, the finance ledger records one ownerless $1.00 mechanic wage before ride and facility upkeep. If cash is insufficient at that instant, employment ends with no negative balance or debt. Dismissing and rehiring resets the partial wage interval; neither action changes a ride's operating settings. The finance totals, park summary, ledger rows and current cost rate include wages separately from ride and facility upkeep.

A hired mechanic services an inspection-due coaster or carousel when it is closed, stationary and has no passenger still on the ride. The mechanic does not close or reopen rides automatically. The already supported manual Service ride action remains available. No spatial staff path, patrol area, inspection duration, breakdown type or safety accident is asserted by this stage.

Strict park save schema/content advance to **27/27**. Version 26 and older saves migrate to no mechanic, zero wage progress and zero wage history. Current saves require both mechanic fields and the wage total field. Older-schema smuggling of staff state or a wage receipt is rejected. Loaded wage progress is bounded and zero when no mechanic is hired. Archived wages must be whole fixed charges within possible elapsed simulation time; retained wage receipts have no ride or service owner and conserve cash. No external asset, dependency or simulation random draw is added. This development PC is not a performance reference.
