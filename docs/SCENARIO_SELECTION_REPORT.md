# Scenario selection stage — 0.69.0

## Scope

This phase adds a dedicated **Scenarios** pane to the Guests window. It shows sandbox status, authored rules, current progress and outcome before the player commits to a challenge. Meadow Challenge remains available. A second original preset, **Boardwalk Sprint**, combines free rides and both allowed advertising products with a ten-minute deadline. Its 24-arrival goal exceeds the roughly 20 ordinary candidate opportunities in that interval, making an arrival campaign useful. It also requires 12 completed visitor ride visits and $900 simultaneous cash. Success takes precedence if the goal is reached on the deadline tick; otherwise a sticky failure is recorded. The park remains playable after either outcome.

The simulation now derives goals, charging policy and deadline from a saved scenario kind. It rejects a second start, forbidden policy changes, and inconsistent saved outcomes. Boardwalk permits radio, while Meadow forbids it. Save schema/content advances from **38/38 to 39/39**. Active version-38 Meadow saves migrate with no failure state; current saves require the new outcome field and old schemas reject the new preset. The cross-process `write-scenarios` / `verify-scenarios` proof starts Boardwalk through the player button, refuses a second challenge and charging change, then saves before its goal. Independent success and failure futures replay from that one primary save and compare exact results while keeping the primary byte-identical. Windows CI runs this proof.
