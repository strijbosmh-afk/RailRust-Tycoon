# Vibrant park and ride art — 0.58.0

The supplied reference calls for saturated colors, more substantial rides and a denser park surface. This stage changes the game's original mesh art: a vivid grass/water/sand palette, brighter tree foliage and flower patches; a warm timber coaster trestle with physical cross braces and red rail heads; a steeper, striped carousel canopy with open bays and small finials; and stronger service colors with path-facing striped valances on Food and Drink stalls. The player can inspect the artwork through the existing ride focus and zoom controls.

Coaster braces are emitted only when both posts and the center column are clear below the track. The rail and tie geometry still follows sampled grades, curves and joins. The carousel canopy remains inside its reserved 3 × 3 footprint and below its three-step clearance; the open bays preserve views of moving mounts. Service accents stay inside the one-tile footprint. All artwork uses the existing per-face depth, static chunks, selection and preview pipelines. Normal-map flower density rises, while the existing 32 × 32 cap on close-view decorative terrain remains.

The optional `--capture-detail` diagnostic flag frames train, carousel, `service-needs` and `garden` scenes at 350% for visual review. It changes no normal player camera setting or saved state. No third-party or reference assets, new dependency, random draw or park save field are added. Strict save schema/content stay **28/28**. This PC is excluded from performance acceptance.
