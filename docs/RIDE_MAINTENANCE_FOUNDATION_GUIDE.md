# Ride maintenance — 0.55.0

Open Rides [Y] and choose a coaster or carousel. The Operations tab shows an inspection notice after 120 completed guest trips since the last service. A ride that reaches 1,200 trips without service closes to new departures; any trip already in progress finishes and unloads. The ride remains closed until serviced.

To inspect or repair a ride, close it and let its current trip finish. Click **Service ride** in Operations, then reopen it when the usual track, entrance, exit and operating-funds checks pass. You may service a ride before an inspection is due. The action resets its trip count since service and is covered by the existing ride upkeep rather than charging a second fee. The service record is included when you save the park.

This stage adds a deterministic maintenance cycle and the player control for it. Mechanic hiring, wages, walking to service access, inspections on patrol, and distinct breakdown types are planned later. No automatic staff repair is implied by this button.
