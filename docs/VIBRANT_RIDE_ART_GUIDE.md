# Vibrant park and ride art — 0.58.0

The park now uses brighter grass, water, trees and flower beds. The coaster has warm timber trestles with cross bracing and red rail heads. The carousel has a faceted, colorful tent roof with open bays so its moving mounts can still be seen. Food and Drink stands have striped awnings; all four services have stronger identifying colors and a small roof finial.

Use **Find** in a ride panel or the camera zoom controls to examine the new shapes. Ride paint choices in **Rides [Y] → Colors** still recolor the coaster structure and supports and the carousel structure, supports and vehicles. Construction previews, selection outlines and all four camera rotations follow the same physical shapes.

This is an original procedural art pass inspired by the supplied reference's saturated palette and visual density. It adds no copied sprites, interface art, audio or branding. Park play, prices, routes and strict save format **28/28** are unchanged.
