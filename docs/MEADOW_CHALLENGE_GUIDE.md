# Meadow Challenge — 0.68.0

Open **Guests [L] → Visitor flow** and choose **Start Meadow Challenge**. You can begin in a new or existing sandbox park. The panel then counts progress from the moment you start: welcome **20 new visitors**, complete **5 visitor ride trips**, and have at least **$750.00 park cash** at the same time. The panel marks the challenge **COMPLETED** when all three goals are met during a running simulation tick. The completion stays recorded if cash later falls.

The challenge switches to **Free entry**. Ride tickets remain chargeable. The charging rule stays locked and **radio ads cannot be bought**. Local flyers are allowed if you want faster visitor candidates. If a radio campaign is already running, wait for it to expire before starting. Existing arrivals, completed rides, and cash are not a head start on the first two goals; the arrival and ride counts begin at zero. Changing the charging rule, starting radio, or starting the challenge twice gives a notice and leaves the park unchanged. A challenge cannot be cancelled or changed back to sandbox in this version, so use a separate save before starting if you want to keep an unconstrained park.

To attract guests, set a connected public-path entry, enable arrivals, keep the park open, and connect an open ride to a queue and exit path. The simulation needs to run; Space pauses time and progress. Ride visits by guests who later leave still count. Cash includes borrowed funds, so the cash goal is a balance target rather than a profit target.

The challenge state and completion are saved in strict park schema/content **38/38**. Version-37 and older saves load as sandbox parks. Older game executables cannot open version-38 saves. Your playable park primary and `.bak` saves live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`; close the game and copy both when moving to another PC. The shared OneDrive source checkout is described in [RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md](../RESUME_DEVELOPMENT.md). New games still start flat with the starter coaster station at ground level.
