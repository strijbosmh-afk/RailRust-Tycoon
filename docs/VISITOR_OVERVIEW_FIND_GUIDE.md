# Park-wide visitor snapshot and Find

Open **Guests [L]**, then **Park stats**. The dashboard counts active visitors in three satisfaction bands: low 0–39, mixed 40–69 and high 70–100. Separate bars count guests whose hunger, thirst or toilet urgency is at least 70. The number remains visible beside every bar, so the view does not depend on color. One guest may appear in more than one urgent-need bar. The bottom line shows visitors riding, queueing, seeking/using services and lost. These are live current counts, not historical trends.

Choose **Guest list** to return to individual inspection or **Visitor flow** for arrivals and departures. The views only read park state. Pausing the game keeps the snapshot still; resuming updates it as visitors change.

To locate someone, choose a guest in the list and press **Find** beside their number. The guest window closes, the camera zooms to their current path or ride seat, and a gold ring/marker follows that visitor. Camera rotation and normal guest movement keep the marker attached. If the visitor leaves the park, the marker clears. The guest list remains the way to inspect their details; map clicks still target paths and objects beneath visitors.

This stage adds no new serialized field. Saves remain strict schema/content **24/24** under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`. Copy primary and `.bak` together with the game closed when moving progress to another PC.
