Crossroad paving follows deck connections, and waterfront paths can receive
timber decking. The first intact rectangular ornamental pond on maps up to
64 tiles wide animates a fountain when its center is unobstructed. Water must
be level, at least four tiles in each direction, and bordered by sand.

Tracked catalogue stations have pitched roofs and separate model palettes.
The Pirate Ship, Tea Cups and Observation Tower have distinct platforms.
Custom ride paint remains available. These are shared renderer changes
and use the existing build requirements and save schema.

Use the landscape, catalogue and demo captures to compare the results.
