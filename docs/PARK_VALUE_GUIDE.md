# Recorded park and company book value

Open **Finance [N]** and cycle **Ride cashflows → Park summary → Loans → Park value**. The report has one page and is read-only. Viewing it does not pause the park; **Space** pauses activity and interest.

| Figure | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded park value | Remaining paid track basis + installed carousel basis + installed facility basis |
| Gross assets | Cash + recorded park value |
| Liabilities | Outstanding loan principal + accrued unpaid whole-cent interest |
| Company book value | Gross assets − liabilities |

Paid basis means the actual recorded construction cost of an asset still present. Closed rides and physically present track remnants retain their basis, even when the circuit is faulted. Drafts are not paid assets. A demolished asset leaves this report; its costs and refund remain in finance history. Land, paths, gates and free legacy construction have no recorded price here and receive no invented value.

Borrowing increases cash and loan principal equally, so it does not create company book value or operating profit. Repaying principal reduces both. Accrued whole-cent interest reduces company value when it becomes due; paying it later reduces cash and the liability equally, without subtracting it twice. Sub-cent interest stays in the loan account and affects value after it reaches a whole cent.

Buying a paid asset exchanges cash for recorded basis. Demolition refunds only 50% of paid basis, so demolition can reduce company value. A company can have negative book value while still having cash. Ride/service operating profit and lifetime cashflow remain separate reports.

These are recorded book values. They do not estimate a sale price, earning capacity, visitor demand, depreciation, park rating or the value of unpriced assets. Save schema/content stays **21/21**; the report adds no saved state or new simulation rules. Loans and interest retain the [existing rules](LOANS_GUIDE.md).
