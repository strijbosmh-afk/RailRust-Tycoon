# Guest statistics and visitor looks

Open **Guests [L]** to inspect individual visitors. The left list shows stable guest numbers, current activity and satisfaction; its colored circle matches each visitor's shirt in the park. Use **Up/Down** or the row buttons to choose a guest, and **PgUp/PgDn** or the page buttons to browse. The selected visitor's larger swatch appears beside their number.

The four meters show exact values out of 100. A high **Satisfaction** value is good; high **Hunger**, **Thirst** or **Toilet** values mean urgent needs. Green, amber and red mark the current range, while the number remains visible without color. Below them are activity, wallet and spending, comfortable ticket and queue limits, time already waited, preferred ride/challenge, completed trips, services and reviews. The thought and selected ride's affordability and challenge fit are grouped separately. These values update as the game advances; **Space** pauses it.

Visitors now have twelve repeatable looks with different original shirt, skin and hair colors, hair silhouettes and shoulder bags. Their look comes from their stable guest ID, so changing camera rotation or loading a save does not reroll it. A lost visitor still wears the orange warning shirt. These are small isometric figures, not individually selectable map targets; use the guest list for inspection.

This stage changes presentation only. Park saves remain strict schema/content **24/24** and continue to use the same primary and `.bak` slots under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parkbound\saves`.
