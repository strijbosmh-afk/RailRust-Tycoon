# Ride colors — 0.45.0

Open **Rides [Y]**, choose a ride, then select **Colors**. The three controls change Structure, Supports and Vehicles independently. Use < and > to cycle through Classic, Coral, Lagoon, Sunflower, Meadow, Sky, Violet, Charcoal and Cream. Each choice has a named swatch.

Painting is free and can be done while the ride is open or occupied. It does not change fares, running costs, physical shape, capacity, passengers, finances, ratings or guest choices. Coaster structure colors cover rails and track bed; support colors cover columns; vehicle colors cover car bodies. Carousel colors cover the deck/cap, mast/trim and the moving seats/horses. Gates, queues, passenger clothing, wheels, wooden ties, lift chain and brakes keep their existing functional colors.

**Show in park** closes the window and focuses the selected ride, keeping pause/speed intact. **Restore classic colors** resets only that ride. Save the park after repainting to keep your choices; F5 is available after closing the ride window. Other rides keep their own palettes. Demolished rides retain their historical appearance record, and replacement ride IDs start Classic. Faulted track remnants retain their scheme for repair and their visible fallback rails use the chosen structure color.

Older saves load with their original Classic appearance. New saves use schema/content **23/23**, with explicit migrations from versions 1–22. Older 0.44 and earlier readers refuse these new saves; keep an old copy if you want to return to an earlier build. The sound settings file remains separate and unchanged.

Paint belongs to the installed ride. Named/imported/exported track designs still store geometry in format 1; they do not copy a ride's paint scheme. Replacing a coaster layout retains that ride's identity and colors. New construction ghosts keep their normal validity tint.

This is the first ride-customization slice. Palette presets, more vehicle parts, saved design themes, ride naming, editable scenery and spatial audio remain later work. All existing original music, sound controls and park-management features remain available.
