# Graphics and sound quality overhaul — 0.53.0

This stage expands the original procedural art and audio systems without changing authoritative park state. Normal-size map roofs gain clipped grass and sand details; water/land boundaries gain two shallow shoreline bands, mapped through all four rotations. Exposed soil cliffs gain a lit lip and small cracks. Public-path wood grain, fasteners and tree-crown highlights improve the material read at close zoom. Decoration uses the terrain/object face's own depth and stays outside picking. The 256×256 diagnostic renderer retains its simpler geometry by capping the new close-view details at 32×32.

The original score adds a second twenty-second melody phrase, a four-chord harmonic bed, shaped bass and a crowd-responsive arpeggio to the established meadow melody. The mixer positions coaster and carousel buses in stereo from their projected world locations relative to the camera, with distance attenuation and per-sample smoothing to prevent abrupt shifts. Coaster sound tracks the current leading car. Build and removal cues gain a brief shaped transient and harmonic color. Master, music, ambience, rides and interface controls keep their existing independent settings and bounded, nonblocking Windows PCM output.

No imported bitmap, audio recording, melody, font or new dependency is added. The sound settings file remains strict version 1 with a 4 KiB cap; park saves remain strict **25/25**. The visual details do not alter terrain or object collision, height, selection or navigation. Existing save compatibility and portable Windows distribution are preserved. This development PC is excluded as a performance reference; subjective listening on a target PC and native input remain acceptance work.
